How the numbers are worked out

    
    
      
  • Net sales = invoice net amounts (excluding 15% VAT) minus credit notes, after removing duplicate invoice numbers.
    • 
      
  • Customer money in bank = credits that are buyer transfers or cash deposits. Loans, owner top-ups, transfers between own accounts and tax refunds are left out.
    • 
      
  • Cash-verified sales: for each buyer, what the invoice file says was paid is compared with what that buyer actually transferred. Cash buyers are compared, as a group, with branch cash deposits.
    • 
      
  • DSO (days sales outstanding) = open receivables ÷ last 90 days of gross sales × 90.
    • 
      
  • Days late = paid date minus due date, averaged by invoice value.
    • 
      
  • Borrowing base = open, not-too-overdue invoices from buyers who pay reliably and whose payments show in the bank, less recent credit notes, capped per buyer, times the advance rate, capped again by monthly verified collections.
    • 
      
  • Scorecard thresholds are my own first guesses, set out in the README, and should be replaced by the partner bank's credit policy.
